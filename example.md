# OrderPrintex Prepress & Artwork Guidelines

Welcome to the OrderPrintex digital file preparation and printing guide.

## 1. Technical Print Transfer Specifications

- **Optimal Resolution**: 300 DPI at 100% final output size.
- **Color Mode**: CMYK (U.S. Web Coated SWOP v2 or GRACoL2006). For neon and high-chroma artwork, sRGB is accepted.
- **Backgrounds**: Transparent PNG or vector PDF/AI. Do NOT leave white bounding rectangles unless white ink is intended.
- **Minimum Line Weight**: 
  - Positive Print (colored line on transparent background): 0.5pt (0.18mm)
  - Negative Knockout (transparent gap in solid colored area): 1.0pt (0.35mm)
- **Minimum Typography**: 
  - Sans-serif fonts: 6pt minimum
  - Serif or script fonts: 8pt minimum

## 2. Heat Press Instructions (DTF Garment Transfers)

1. **Pre-Press**: Pre-heat garment for 5 seconds to eliminate moisture and wrinkles.
2. **First Press**: 
   - 150°C – 160°C (302°F – 320°F)
   - Medium to firm pressure (4 to 5 bar / 50–60 PSI)
   - Press for 12–15 seconds
3. **Peeling**: Wait until transfer film is completely cold (Cold Peel). Peel smoothly at a 45-degree angle.
4. **Post-Press (Finishing)**: Cover graphic with parchment paper or Teflon sheet and press for 5 seconds at 150°C to lock adhesive into the weave.

## 3. UV DTF Application (Hard Surface Decals)

- **Surfaces**: Glass, metal, rigid plastic, ceramic, wood, and acrylic.
- **Application**: Clean surface with isopropyl alcohol. Peel backing film (A), place decal firmly on dry surface, rub with squeegee or thumb to bond adhesive, then peel clear carrier film (B) slowly. No heat press required.
