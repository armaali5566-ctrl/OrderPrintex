# Direct UV Flatbed Printing Services | OrderPrintex

- **Canonical URL**: <https://orderprintex.com/services/uv-printing>
- **Description**: Our direct UV printing service utilizes advanced flatbed printers to apply UV-curable inks directly onto rigid substrates. Unlike UV DTF stickers that are transferred via carrier film, flatbed UV printing deposits ink droplets directly onto the substrate, instantly cured by high-intensity UV LED lamps. Ideal for corporate awards, acrylic plaques, metal signage, wooden coasters, phone cases, promotional gifting, and industrial instrument panels with optional raised gloss varnish.
