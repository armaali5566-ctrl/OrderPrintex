## Our Operational Heritage

Founded with a mission to bring world-class digital garment and surface decoration technologies to Indian brands and print studios, OrderPrintex combines precision Japanese printheads, European TPU adhesives, and in-house prepress color calibration.

## Factory Metrics & Infrastructure

- **Heritage**: Over 10 years of prepress and textile printing experience.
- **Technology Focus**: Industrial DTF roll-to-roll and UV DTF cold transfers.
- **Prepress Guarantee**: Every customer file is manually audited for DPI, alpha channels, and color gamut before printing.
- **Pan-India Dispatch**: Daily shipments via Blue Dart, DTDC, Delhivery, and express cargo.
