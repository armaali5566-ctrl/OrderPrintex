## Executive Overview

OrderPrintex is a high-capacity industrial digital printing facility based in New Delhi, India. We deliver commercial-grade Direct-to-Film (DTF) apparel transfers, UV DTF 3D tactile decals, custom t-shirt manufacturing, and corporate swag kits. We operate on a bespoke B2B Enquiry-to-Quotation workflow with automated prepress inspection and pan-India express dispatch.

## Core Manufacturing Capabilities

1. **Direct-to-Film (DTF) Transfers**: Continuous 22-inch roll printing with dual Epson i3200 printheads, ultra-dense white ink backing, and automated TPU adhesive curing.
2. **UV DTF Decals**: Instant cold-peel multi-surface transfer stickers with 3D spot gloss varnish for drinkware, metal, glass, and acrylic.
3. **Custom T-Shirt Printing**: Premium 180 GSM bio-washed combed cotton, 240 GSM oversized streetwear, and sports polyester with photographic detail.
4. **Corporate Swag & Promotional Kits**: Turnkey client onboarding boxes, customized insulated drinkware, diaries, and executive presentation items.

## Machinery & Fleet

- **Industrial Micro-Piezo Dual-Head DTF Printers** (Textile Printing): Dual Epson i3200 micro-piezo heads laying down high-density white underbase and CMYK textile pigments simultaneously. — Photorealistic 1440x2880 DPI resolution with ultra-smooth gradient transitions and flawless solid color fill on dark garments.
- **Automatic Powder Shaker & Infrared Conveyor Oven** (Transfer Curing): Automated roll-to-roll powdering unit with electrostatic vibration and calibrated infrared heat tunnels. — Guarantees uniform TPU adhesive distribution and complete polyurethane polymerization for 50+ wash longevity.
- **Multi-Axis UV DTF Hybrid Flatbed & Roll System** (UV Hard Surfaces): UV LED curing system printing opaque white, CMYK, and high-build clear spot varnish onto release film. — Creates waterproof, scratchproof 3D tactile embossed decals for glass, tumblers, wood, and electronics.
- **Digital Precision Optical Contour Cutting Plotters** (Finishing & Decals): High-speed cutting plotters with optical registration cameras for precision blade contouring. — Flawless kiss-cut and die-cut borders with sub-millimeter registration accuracy on vinyl and holographic stocks.
- **Industrial Pneumatic Auto-Open Heat Presses** (Garment Pressing): Digital dual-platen pneumatic presses with microprocessor temperature control and consistent pneumatic pressure. — Ensures 100% mechanical fiber bonding of transfers without hot spots, scorching, or uneven adhesion.
- **X-Rite Prepress Color Spectrophotometers** (Prepress & Quality): Spectrophotometric color management instruments calibrating ICC profiles for CMYK and Pantone reproduction. — Guarantees consistent color fidelity between digital client mockups and physical press-ready output.
