## B2B Commercial Pricing Structure

OrderPrintex offers transparent per-meter and per-unit tiered pricing. As print volume increases, per-unit rates decrease significantly.

### DTF Transfer Rolls (22-Inch Width)
- **1 to 5 Linear Meters**: Standard retail/sample tier.
- **6 to 20 Linear Meters**: Brand & studio discount tier (10% - 15% off).
- **21 to 50 Linear Meters**: Bulk trade tier (20% - 25% off).
- **50+ Linear Meters**: Master wholesale roll pricing (up to 35% off).
