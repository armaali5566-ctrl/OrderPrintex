## 6. Prepress Engineering & Artwork Standards

To guarantee press-ready color precision and crisp edges, all customer files must adhere to our prepress technical guidelines:

- **Target DPI**: Minimum 300 DPI at 100% scale. Lower resolutions cause pixelation and blur along transfer borders.
- **Alpha Transparency**: Backgrounds must be 100% transparent PNG or vector shapes. Any semi-transparent or white background pixels will print as solid opaque white ink.
- **Color Profiling**: CMYK color space is preferred (US Web Coated SWOP or Coated FOGRA39). For rich, deep blacks on garments, specify `C:60 M:40 Y:40 K:100`.
- **Font Outlining**: Convert all typography to vector paths/curves (`Type > Create Outlines` in Adobe Illustrator).
- **Minimum Vector Stroke**: Maintain minimum stroke weight of 0.5pt (0.18mm) for positive lines and 0.75pt (0.26mm) for negative reverse lines.

### Q6. [DTF Printing] What is DTF printing and which fabrics does it bond to?

DTF (Direct-to-Film) is a cutting-edge digital garment decoration technique. Artwork is printed onto specialized PET release film using high-density CMYK textile inks plus a solid white underbase, followed by a layer of hot-melt polyurethane adhesive powder cured under heat. DTF transfers apply flawlessly to virtually any fabric: 100% cotton, combed cotton, 100% polyester, poly-cotton blends, fleece, denim, nylon, canvas, and performance spandex.

### Q7. [DTF Printing] What is the recommended heat press setting for DTF transfers?

For optimal washability and soft-hand feel: 1) Pre-press garment 3-5 seconds to remove moisture. 2) Transfer Press at 160°C (320°F) for 15 seconds under medium-to-firm pressure (40-50 PSI / 4-5 bar). 3) Cold Peel: Allow the transfer to cool completely to room temperature, then peel PET film smoothly. 4) Post-press: Cover graphic with Teflon or silicone parchment paper and press for 5 seconds to seal the transfer into the weave.

### Q8. [DTF Printing] How do I wash DTF-printed garments to ensure 50+ wash longevity?

Turn garments inside-out, wash in cold or lukewarm water (max 30°C / 85°F) with mild detergent, avoid bleach or harsh spot removers, tumble dry on gentle low heat or hang dry in shade, and never iron directly on the printed graphic.

### Q9. [UV DTF Printing] What is UV DTF printing and how does it work?

UV DTF is an innovative cold-transfer technology engineered specifically for hard, non-porous surfaces. Using piezoelectric printheads, the printer deposits white ink, vibrant CMYK inks, and a high-gloss 3D tactile clear varnish, cured instantaneously by ultraviolet LED lamps. Prints are applied directly to products without heat presses or ovens: peel, align, rub firmly, and peel away the carrier film.

### Q10. [UV DTF Printing] Which surfaces are compatible with UV DTF transfer stickers?

UV DTF stickers bond permanently to clean, rigid, and smooth or lightly contoured materials including glassware, stainless steel tumblers, aluminum water flasks, ceramic mugs, plastics, acrylics, cell phone cases, rigid electronics, lacquered wood, and cosmetic packaging.

### Q11. [UV DTF Printing] Are UV DTF decals waterproof, scratch-resistant, and dishwasher safe?

Yes. Because inks are cross-linked under high-intensity UV LED light and sealed with a heavy clear spot varnish layer, UV DTF stickers are 100% waterproof, UV-resistant, and scratch-resistant under normal handling. For maximum longevity on drinkware, hand-washing with a soft sponge is recommended.
